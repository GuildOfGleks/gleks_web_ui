gog-radio-group

Radio Group

A native <input type="radio"> group driven by an options array, with a two-way bindable selection and a `ControlValueAccessor` implementation — mutual exclusivity and arrow-key navigation between options come for free from the shared name the group gives its native radios.

"Enterprise" is disabled at the option level (disabled: true on that option), independent of the group's own disabled input.

Orientation

orientation="vertical" (default) stacks options in a column; "horizontal" lays them out in a row.

Sizes & disabled

Full width & label-less

fullWidth stretches the group (and, in horizontal orientation, spaces its options) to fill its container instead of hugging its content. ariaLabel names a group that has no visible label at all — same pattern as gog-select/gog-multiselect elsewhere on this site.

Reactive forms

Bound via [formControl] only — no [(value)] — proving the ControlValueAccessor wiring on its own. With errorDisplay="auto", the group decides for itself when to show the error (once touched and invalid) — pick nothing, tab away, and it appears.
