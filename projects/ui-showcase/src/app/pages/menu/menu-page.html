items: plain, with an icon and a hint, disabled

An item is your own <button gogMenuItem>, so its content is yours — an icon, a label, a shortcut. A native disabled item is skipped by the arrow keys and cannot be pressed. Hover and the focused item are live; the trigger is your own <button gogButton>. Put the trigger on a native button, not on the gog-button component: there its ARIA lands on the component's host and never reaches the real button — filed.

keyboard, and gogClosed

Focus the trigger. Enter, Space or ArrowDown open with the first item focused, ArrowUp with the last; Home and End jump; disabled items are skipped. gogClosed fires however the menu closed — an item, Escape, Tab or a click outside.
