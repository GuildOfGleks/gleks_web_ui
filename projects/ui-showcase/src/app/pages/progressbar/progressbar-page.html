size × mode

determinate fills to value; buffer adds a second, paler tier behind it for what has been fetched but not reached; indeterminate sweeps a segment for work whose length is not known. Under prefers-reduced-motion the sweep stops and the bar is drawn striped instead, which still reads as "working".

variant × mode

showValue

Off by default, because a bar usually sits under a label that already says what it measures. On, the percentage prints beside the track in tabular figures, so the bar does not shift as the number changes. It is never shown in indeterminate mode, which has no number.

where the fill ends, with and without colour

With showValue off, the boundary between fill and track is the value, and colour alone cannot carry it: the five fills straddle mid-luminance in every theme, so no single track colour clears WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 against all of them. Two hairlines at the fill's leading edge do — one dark, one light, so whichever a fill sits close to, the other stands out. The right-hand column is the left one with hue taken away; every boundary is still there.

a value that moves, and one that overshoots

A bar driven straight from loaded / total overshoots on the last chunk often enough that it is clamped here instead of being allowed to break the layout. Step past 100 or below 0: the bar stops at the end and aria-valuenow with it, while the number you passed is kept as you passed it. The fill moves with a short transition, which reduced motion turns off.

every bar needs a name

ariaLabel is empty by default, and a progress bar with no name is announced as "progress bar, 62 percent" — of what, nobody says. Pass ariaLabel, or, when a visible label already sits beside the bar, point at it with aria-labelledby written on the host, as the last row does: the component binds no aria-labelledby of its own, so yours reaches the element with the role. A <label for> cannot do it — a progress bar is not a form field.
