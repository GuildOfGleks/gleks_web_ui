setting × host shape

rippleCentred starts every wave from the middle, as keyboard activation always does — Enter and Space carry no coordinates. rippleDisabled removes the listeners and the host class, not just the paint. A host that is disabled or aria-disabled="true" does not answer a press it will not act on. Under prefers-reduced-motion nothing ripples at all: it is the most purely decorative motion in the library.

on the element that paints the surface

The wash lives in a layer that takes its corners from the host with border-radius: inherit. Put the directive on a wrapper whose child draws the rounded background, and the layer inherits the wrapper's radius — usually none — and squares off at the corners. Both pills below are wrapped; only the second has the directive on the pill itself. Press near a corner of each.

it does not clip a badge

The obvious ripple sets overflow: hidden on its host, which would cut off a gogBadge that sits outside the host's box on purpose. This one clips its own layer instead, so the two directives share an element.

--gog-ripple-color, --gog-ripple-opacity and the two durations are read where the host sits, so one surface can differ from the rest.

Writing gogRipple on your own element is already a per-element decision, so the config does not apply to it — rippleDisabled takes it back.
