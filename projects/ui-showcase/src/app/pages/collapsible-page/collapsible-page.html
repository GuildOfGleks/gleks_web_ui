gog-collapsible

Collapsible

A headless expand/collapse primitive — inline, not a portal like gog-select's panel. It owns no markup: gogCollapsibleTrigger and gogCollapsibleContent attach to whatever elements you project, so the trigger can be a button, a whole header row, or nothing at all if you drive [(open)] from outside.

Overview

A plain button trigger and a list of links standing in for panel content.

States

Closed and open are just the starting value of [(open)] — both stay fully interactive. disabled blocks the trigger's click handler entirely and marks it aria-disabled.

collapseOnFocusOut

Open the panel, then Tab through its links. With collapseOnFocusOut="true", tabbing past the last link (or clicking the button below, outside the panel entirely) closes it automatically. Tabbing between the trigger and the links — anywhere still inside — leaves it open. Off by default, since plenty of consumers want the panel to stay open regardless of where focus goes next.

Access control — disable vs. hide a child

gog-collapsible is headless, so per-item access control is just ordinary bindings on the content you project — no special API needed.
