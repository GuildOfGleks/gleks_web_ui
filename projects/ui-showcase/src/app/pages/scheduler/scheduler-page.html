view="month": multi-day bars, and "+N more" in a popover

An event that spans days keeps one row across the week, so its pieces join into one bar; the title is drawn where the bar starts and again where a week row begins. Rows are ordered: all-day and multi-day events first, longest and earliest on top, then timed events by start — and a timed event fills a row a bar left free. A cell draws monthEventLimit rows (3 by default), and a fuller day ends in "+N more", which lists the whole day in a gog-popover (moreEventsAction="day" opens the day view instead).

[monthEventLimit]="5" — or GOG_CONFIG.scheduler.monthEventLimit for the whole app

The cell is as tall as its rows: five here, so a day with five events shows all of them and only a sixth would end the cell in "+N more". The words come from GOG_CONFIG.labels.moreEvents, a formatter, so a language can put the number where its grammar wants it.

view="day", dayStartHour="8", dayEndHour="18", slotDuration="15"

Overlapping events share the column; one that overlaps nothing takes all of it. An event shorter than a slot is drawn a slot tall. The red line is now, in today's column only.

[readonly]="false"

Right-click an empty slot or day for New event, or an event for Move, Change end and Delete. Move and Change end then wait for the place: press the slot or day it goes to — with the pointer, or arrow to it and press Enter — and Escape cancels. A move keeps the event's length; dropped on a day in the month view it keeps its time of day too. This page applies every output to its own array, which is all the scheduler asks of an app.

one event, timeZone="America/New_York" and timeZone="Asia/Tokyo"

The same instant, 13:00 UTC tomorrow, drawn in two zones: 09:00 in New York, 22:00 in Tokyo. A slot pressed in either emits the instant that clock time means there.

host attributes

keyboard

One day holds the tab stops: its cell (month) or its header, all-day cell and one slot (week and day), and its events. ←/→ move a day, ↑/↓ a week in the month and a slot in the hour grid, Home/End go to the ends of the week (or of the day's slots), PageUp/PageDown change the period. Moving past what is drawn moves the view. Enter or Space presses — a day, a slot or an event — through each button's own click. Every slot and event is a button named in full ("Design review, Monday, October 12, 10:00 – 11:30 AM").
