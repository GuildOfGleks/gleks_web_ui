[(open)] and the header row

Click anywhere on the header row, or Tab to the toggle and press Enter or Space. The heading stays a heading: the toggle is its own button, named by the heading, with its hit area stretched across the row. open is ignored while collapsible is off — a panel that cannot collapse is always open.
