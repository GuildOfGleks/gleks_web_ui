gog-alert

Alert

A persistent, in-flow message. Unlike a toast it has no timer, no queue and no overlay — it renders where you write it and stays until your app removes it.

Severities

@for (severity of severities; track severity) { The severity chooses the edge colour and the glyph. accent is the library's own colour and claims nothing, which is the default. }

Dismissible

dismissed is pressed, not removed — the alert stays in the DOM and the app decides. That is deliberate: a component that deleted itself would take the focused element with it.

@if (showDismissible()) { The card issuer declined the charge. No money has left your account. } @else { Bring the alert back }

Icons

Each severity brings its own glyph. iconName overrides it, and null removes it for a message whose words already carry the meaning.

An iconName overrides the severity's own glyph. [iconName]="null" suppresses the icon entirely. A projected gogAlertIcon template replaces it with your own markup.

Long content

The body wraps and the icon stays at the top, so a paragraph does not centre its glyph against the middle of the text.
