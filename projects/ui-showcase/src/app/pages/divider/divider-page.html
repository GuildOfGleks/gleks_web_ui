A label's text is read after the separator — in Chrome it is in the accessibility tree as ordinary text — but the separator itself carries no name. ARIA treats a separator's contents as presentational, so a browser may drop the label; naming the separator from its label would make it dependable, and is filed. Until then, do not put words a reader must hear only in a divider's label.
