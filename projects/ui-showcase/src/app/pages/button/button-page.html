The label is hidden under the spinner with opacity: 0, so the button keeps its width and its name: a screen reader hears "Save, button, busy". Until 21.15.0 it was hidden with visibility: hidden, which also takes text out of the accessibility tree, and every loading button was announced as just "button". The spinner itself is aria-hidden inside a button — aria-busy already says it.
