size × state

There is no indeterminate here and there should not be: a setting is on or it is off, and "partly on" is not an answer it can give. The track, the thumb and the label scale from --gog-toggle-<size>-* — this control has its own scale, unlike gog-checkbox and gog-radio-group, which share one; only the padding is borrowed from theirs.

the label, and which side it sits on

The control is a <label> around a real <input type="checkbox">, so the text is the accessible name with no for to wire up and the whole row is the hit target. labelPosition="start" puts the text first, which is what a settings list wants: the switches then line up on one edge instead of after labels of different lengths.

fullWidth, and what a settings list looks like

Off by default, so a switch is as wide as its label. Turned on it fills its container, and with labelPosition="start" that is the row every settings screen is made of — label at one edge, switch at the other, the whole line pressable.

onLabel and offLabel — text inside the track

The one thing a checkbox genuinely cannot do, and what makes a switch readable at a glance in a dense list. Both labels are always in the DOM, so the track is as wide as the wider of the two and cannot jump as the switch flips — the inactive one is faded, not removed. Keep them short, and do not put the state's meaning only there: they are aria-hidden, because the role already announces on and off.

a switch with no visible label

ariaLabel names it when the surrounding row already says what it is — a table cell, a toolbar. It is the only case that input applies to.

[(checked)]

A switch takes effect immediately — there is no Save button between the press and the setting, which is the behavioural half of the difference from a checkbox. That makes checkedChange the place to do the work, and it means a switch belongs to something that can actually apply at once.

input attributes

Read from the live DOM. The role is the only ARIA on the element — the state is the input's own checked property, which is why no aria-checked appears here and why Space, form submission and a :checked selector all keep working. The track, the thumb and the two state labels are aria-hidden: they are the painting, and painting it differently is the entire difference from gog-checkbox.

when to reach for this and when for a checkbox

A switch answers "is this setting on" and applies at once; a checkbox answers "is this one of the things you selected" and waits for the form around it. That is why this component has no indeterminate, no error line and no group: a list of switches is a list of independent settings, while a list of checkboxes is usually one answer made of several parts.
