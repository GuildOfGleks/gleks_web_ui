gog-progressbar

Progressbar

A horizontal progress indicator with determinate, indeterminate and buffer modes. Values are clamped to 0–100 rather than trusted, because a bar driven from loaded / total overshoots on the last chunk often enough to matter.

Run Stop

Indeterminate

Reports no aria-valuenow at all — that omission is what marks it indeterminate to assistive tech. The animation is replaced by a static stripe under prefers-reduced-motion.

Buffer

A second, lighter level ahead of the fill — preloaded but not yet played.

Where the fill ends, in greyscale

The boundary between fill and track is the value — showValue is false by default, so nothing else states it. It cannot be carried by the two colours themselves: the five fills straddle mid-luminance in every palette, so a track dark enough for the amber warning lands on the blue info, and no single track colour clears WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 against all five in any shipped theme but one. Two hairlines at the fill's leading edge do — the ink line against the track measures 3.25:1 to 9.57:1 across the eleven themes, and whichever tone a fill sits close to, the other one does not.

Tick the box to render this panel the way a reader with achromatopsia sees it. Before 21.10.0 three of these five bars lost their boundary almost entirely here; the tick is what is left when hue is taken away.
