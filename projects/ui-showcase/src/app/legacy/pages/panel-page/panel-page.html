gog-panel

Panel

A titled region of a page — a settings section, a dashboard area, a form group. Unlike gog-card it is a real landmark (role="region" named by its heading), it can collapse through the existing gog-collapsible primitive, and its surface is never itself a link: controls live inside a panel.

Variants and sizes

Same three variants as gog-card, from the shared GogSurfaceVariant type — elevated is the default here, because a page region is usually the raised thing on the page.

Collapsible

collapsible adds the toggle and composes gog-collapsible under it — the open/close state, the id wiring and the animation are the library's existing ones. The heading stays a heading: the toggle is its own button, named by the heading through aria-labelledby, with its hit area stretched across the header row so clicking the title still works.

Theming

gog-panel shares its token prefix with the foundation surface tier on purpose: --gog-panel-radius, --gog-panel-shadow and the border pair are the same tokens dialogs and dropdown panels read, so a theme's idea of a raised surface reaches this component for free. Everything else — background, padding, heading, toggle, footer — is the component's own --gog-panel-* family.
