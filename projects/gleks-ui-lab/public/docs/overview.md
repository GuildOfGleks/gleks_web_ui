# @guildofgleks/ui

**Guild of Gleks UI** is a component library for building consistent, accessible web
interfaces quickly — without fighting a heavyweight design system or writing every
button and dialog from scratch.

## What it gives you

- A curated set of ready-made building blocks — 30 of them: buttons, form controls,
  date pickers, dialogs, tables, tabs, navigation and feedback components.
- A consistent visual language across your whole product, out of the box.
- Effortless light/dark theming that adapts to your brand, plus three ready-made
  presets you can drop in as-is.
- One place to set app-wide defaults, so a house style isn't repeated on every
  instance.
- Accessible by default — keyboard navigation, focus states and screen-reader
  support are built in, not bolted on.
- Zero runtime dependencies: native `Date`, no CDK, no date library, no theming
  engine to learn.

## Who it's for

Teams who want their product to look and feel like one coherent thing, without
maintaining a design system from scratch or adopting a large third-party framework
just to get a button and a dropdown.

## Where to go next

- **Getting Started** — add the library to your project in a couple of minutes.
- **Theming** — adapt colors, spacing and typography to your brand, or pick a preset.
- **Global Configuration** — set app-wide defaults once instead of per instance.
- **Components** (left sidebar) — browse every component the library ships with.
