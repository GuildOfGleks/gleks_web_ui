Accessibility

The host carries role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin / aria-valuemax and, in determinate and buffer mode, aria-valuenow plus a readable aria-valuetext.

In indeterminate mode it reports no aria-valuenow at all — that omission is precisely what marks it indeterminate. Reporting 0 instead would announce "0 percent" forever. Its animation is also replaced by a static stripe under prefers-reduced-motion.

An edge marker you can turn on. With showValue off — the default — the boundary between fill and track is the only thing stating the value, and WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of the part of a graphic that carries its meaning. Fill against track cannot always carry it: 51 of the 55 shipped fill/track combinations are below 3:1, because the five fills straddle mid-luminance and no single track colour clears all of them in any theme but primeng. So the bar can mark where the fill ends — and, in buffer mode, where the buffer ends — with two hairlines: the theme's ink outermost against the track, the surface colour just inside the fill. Whichever tone a fill sits close to, the other one reads, and the worst case across the eleven themes is 3.25:1.

The marker is off by default — --gog-progressbar-edge-width is 0px. It was drawn on every bar from 21.10.0, and in use the light line read as an outline at the end of the bar rather than as the value, most of all on a dark fill. Set the width to 1px, on :root or on one bar, where the value has to be readable without colour — or turn on showValue instead. The tones are --gog-progressbar-edge-color / -edge-backing-color, and they flip end under dir="rtl".

default — no marker

--gog-progressbar-edge-width: 1px
